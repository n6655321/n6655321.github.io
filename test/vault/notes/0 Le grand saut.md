# A saut et à gambade : l'acte de foi quotidien
It seems to me that the assumption of such objects [mathematical objects] is
quite as legitimate as the assumption of physical bodies and there is quite as
much reason to believe in their existence. They are in the same sense
necessary to obtain a satisfactory system of mathematics as physical bodies
are necessary for a satisfactory theory of our sense perceptions and in both
cases it is impossible to interpret the propositions one wants to assert about
these entities as propositions about the "data", i.e., in the latter case the
actually occurring sense perceptions.22
